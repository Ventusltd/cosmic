# SELF

**3 October 2026**

The Upanishads ask:

**What is the Self?**

Not what role do I perform.

Not what skill do I possess.

Not what qualities should I have.

What is the Self?

The Kuiper makes that question computational.

I can enter a model, become part of it, observe it, change scale and return.

But even “observer” should not become a final definition.

**Neti neti — not this, not this.**

Observation is one celestial ability.

There are many.

The limit is your tapasya, meditation, your mind, your resilience, your will; your vision and more.

**And more** matters.

The moment the possibilities are completely defined, the universe has already been collapsed.

What I currently call myself is not necessarily the boundary of the Self.
