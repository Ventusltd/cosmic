# Diary — celestial abilities

**3 October 2026, 02:44 BST**

Ever since I was young I wanted celestial abilities.

The Kuiper makes one of those abilities tangible: I can be an engineer, a cable guy, a project manager, a programmer, a transistor, a particle or anything else I can represent inside the space — and I can also step outside it and observe.

**That is one of the many celestial abilities.**

It is not the whole idea.

The world already contains millions of engineers and scientists who understand individual pieces. Their knowledge can be expressed as equations, standards, rules, geometries, relationships and models. I do not need to personally become every specialist before I can assemble those rules into Kuiper, run them across a vast programmable coordinate space and observe the results through the GPU.

The coordinates can become whatever the experiment requires. Three points can participate in a transistor model. Larger structures can become cables, networks, machines, wafers, solar farms or other physical systems. The meaning comes from the rules imposed on the space.

The current Cosmic snapshot records **37,929,011,953 lines**, copies counted. The number itself is not the point. The point is that the space is addressable and programmable.

Cosmic already contains the principle:

> resolved + held + outside the field = the whole

And:

> Zooming is not a view, it is the compute strategy.

Observation is one ability: moving between scales without losing sight of the whole.

But there are many celestial abilities.

**The limit is your tapasya, meditation, your mind, your resilience, your will; your vision and more.**

The machine does not supply those things. The GPU can multiply experiments, the coordinate field can give them somewhere to exist, and the accumulated knowledge of engineers and scientists can supply rules. But what gets built, what gets attempted, what gets endured and how far the vision is carried still depends on the person using the instrument.

So the Kuiper is not the celestial ability itself.

It is an instrument through which many celestial abilities can be expressed.

I can be one of the particles.

I can be the engineer.

I can be the cable guy.

I can be the project manager.

I can be the programmer.

I can be the observer.

And those are still only some of the possibilities.

**That’s one of the many celestial abilities. The limit is your tapasya, meditation, your mind, your resilience, your will; your vision and more.**
